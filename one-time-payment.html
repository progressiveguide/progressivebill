One-Time Payment Guide

This page explains when one-time payments make sense and how to complete them with fewer errors.

When to use a one-time payment

  • You prefer manual control each billing cycle.
  • Your payment amount changes and you want to review before paying.
  • You are not ready to set up recurring auto-pay.

Preparation checklist

  • Current billing statement or account record
  • Policy number and billing zip code
  • Payment method details (card or bank)
  • A secure connection and enough time to review entries

Step-by-step process

  1. Go to the official payment entry point from your statement.
  2. Select one-time payment or guest payment path.
  3. Enter amount and account details carefully.
  4. Review due date and total before submitting.
  5. Record confirmation ID and keep receipt evidence.

Confirmation and receipt retention

Keep your confirmation number, timestamp, and screenshot or emailed receipt until your bill history reflects the payment.

Use official statements and billing records as your source of truth. This guide does not place robocalls or automated calls.

Common mistakes and prevention

  • Wrong amount: Compare against latest statement before submitting.
  • Duplicate submissions: Wait for confirmation or pending status before trying again.
  • Missing proof: Save receipt details immediately after payment.
  • Unverified links: Avoid paying through links from unsolicited calls/messages.

FAQ

Is one-time payment the same as autopay?

No. One-time payment is manual for a single billing cycle.
