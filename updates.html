Scam-Avoidance and Contact Safety

This guide does not place automated calls or send robocalls. If you receive an unsolicited call asking for full card details, treat it as suspicious.

  • Verify any payment amount against your official billing statement first.
  • Use account records or printed billing documents before making a payment.
  • Call during normal business hours when you need live help, and avoid sharing sensitive data on unexpected inbound calls.
